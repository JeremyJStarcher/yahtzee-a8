Hi VIC20 addicts!

This is my first release on the VIC20, 
and I really hope you like it =)

This demo part contains a smooth scroll 
(not ROL scroll!) and a Tech-Tech
(that's what I believe it was called on the C64...)

This demo part runs on un-expanded (PAL) VIC20...

Use joystick left and right to control the scroll-
speed (there is no speed limit!)

Use joystick up and down, with or without fire button, 
to change sinus1 speed and distance.

Use Z, C, B and M to change sinus2 speed and distance.
